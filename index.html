How to play?

  • Push the Start Game to get 20 random Pokemons from the PokeApi
  • You and the CPU will be dealt 10 hidden cards each
  • Each round compares the Base Experience (XP) of the active cards.
  • The highest XP wins the round and takes both cards! In case of a tie, the cards are discarded.
  • The game has 30 rounds
  • If the score is the same after 30 rounds it ends in a tie!
